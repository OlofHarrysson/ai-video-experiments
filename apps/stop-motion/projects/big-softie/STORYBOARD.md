Animation uses four distinct running drawings per dog at six pose changes per second, with movement held at twelve spatial updates per second and repeated frames at 24 fps. Fixed artwork is composited with cels. The settling sequence adds seven individually generated paired drawings with authored hold lengths. No optical-flow interpolation or model-generated intermediate video. The return pass uses derived mirrored cels so frame seeking has no transform ambiguity.

Motion references: HyperFrames sine-wave-loop for bounded time-based motion principles and particle-burst for pure ballistic position evaluation. Character-specific pose exposure and ground alignment are authored explicitly; marketing-motion presets do not serve this film.
