Explicit priorities: high production quality, crisp artwork, spatial coherence, thoughtful storytelling, autonomous tool building and iterative review with workflow changes when necessary. Technique under evaluation: generated hand-painted park plate and transparent character cels, deterministic pose animation and camera staging in HyperFrames.
