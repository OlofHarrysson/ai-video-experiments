# Hosted avatar competitors

Research checked: **30 September 2026**. Official product pages and documentation only. No service was purchased or runtime-tested during this research; advertised quality is not a measured result.

## Recommendation for this project

Olof clarified that he wants an **actual controllable 3D character**, which does not have to look like him. Start with a rigged character and an audio-driven animation/rendering pipeline. None of the hosted products below documents delivery of an editable mesh, skeleton, facial blendshapes, and animation curves in the material reviewed. They deliver generated video, even when the result depicts a character with a 3D animation style.

The distinction affects control: a rig exposes camera position, poses, lighting, expressions, and repeatable animation; video generators generally expose a reference image, script/audio, and prompts. Prompted gestures are not equivalent to exact joint or facial control. This is our assessment of fit, not a comparative quality benchmark.

Keep **Hedra** as an inexpensive rendered-video comparison if the project later needs a fast talking-character clip. Keep **HeyGen** as the complete presenter-video benchmark. Neither should replace the 3D pipeline for the current requirement.

## Budget and verification notes

**Google Gemini / Vids:** Google now documents personal avatars built from a face-and-voice recording, used in Gemini Omni videos. The current help page says they are **unavailable in the EEA, Switzerland and United Kingdom** and supported only in English. That excludes the default Sweden-based trial. They are generated video characters rather than an exported 3D rig. [Gemini help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16984474), [Vids announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/gemini-omni-personal-avatars/).

- The **$50 is a total project ceiling**, not authorization for $50 of recurring monthly subscriptions. Keep any optional video comparison to a small prepaid amount or explicit single-month purchase, accounting for tax and voice/image charges.
- If comparing generated video later, reserve roughly **$10–15 for several short renders**, then judge appearance, voice, and lip-sync before expanding. This is a proposed allocation; no money has been spent by this research.
- HeyGen's help-center and developer pages disagree on some 4K and legacy-engine details. The estimates above deliberately use Avatar IV at 720p/1080p; verify the actual job estimate before submission.
- Captions' older indexed API page advertises terms no longer present on the current Mirage API page. Do not use the old $0.05/second or free-credit claim without fresh verification.
- Prices, credit conversion, account eligibility, and output limits should be checked again at the actual purchase or render step. No API key is required merely to continue building the local 3D proof of concept.
