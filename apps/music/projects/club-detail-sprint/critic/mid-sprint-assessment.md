## What has improved

Pressure Study v006 has the clearest compositional proposition: a five-step metallic cell changes into an exposed elastic bass gesture, then remains part of the returned groove. This creates a relationship among events instead of merely changing sound count. The v005→v006 exposed section at cycles 6.5–7.75 gains 10.4 dB in the 30–180 Hz band while its body and presence bands remain essentially unchanged. Low-band levels during the full return are unchanged. The model called the two excerpts exactly identical, which is literally false; failure to notice the added low component does not by itself establish whether it improves the groove.

Negative Space v007 makes a related object change role: whole printed gesture, slowed memory, uneven slices, then whole gesture again. The warmer printed object recovers a lower body that earlier repeated high-pass stages removed. Its opening trigger at five sixteenths places its measured 20 ms RMS peak around 0.616 seconds, between the preceding kick at 0.469 and following bass note at 0.703. The object's significant envelope extends about 1.16 seconds, allowing a softer tail to overlap later events without placing the main attack on them. That supports the intended interaction; it does not prove the mix is compelling.

Latch v002 retains a half-time kick pulse through its middle, differentiating its continuity from the more exposed breaks in the other two candidates. Its rough breathing sound and burred bass answer are a distinct palette from metallic percussion and dub chord material. Current renders require their own screening; the earlier review's description of toy-like wood prompted a replacement with a slower, filtered shuck.

## Remaining artistic question

All three have better authored relationships than the baseline's mostly additive texture, but impressive source architecture is not enough. The shortlist should prefer the two most distinguishable experiences whose signature survives in the full mix. Avoid using the number of events, effect chains, version numbers or clean technical measurements as a proxy for that result.

The strongest test is whether the listener can recognize a recurring object when its role changes. A named technique supplied in the review prompt can cause the model to echo our intention, so neutral descriptions must precede specific attribution questions. The available model's reliable scope remains narrower than its confident prose.
