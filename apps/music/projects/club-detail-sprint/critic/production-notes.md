# Production references and review criteria

2026-10-10. Direction: two original 30-second underground club pieces retaining Undertow's approved bass/drum body, with much more audible detail. No foreground tune or replacement of the approved low end. Research below uses artists' descriptions and a production author's method; it is not an auditory analysis of their commercial recordings.

## Professional methods and our application

**El Choop: make the echo a separate sound.** His direct Ableton interview describes filtering and distorting delay returns, keeping stereo echo narrower than hard ping-pong, and using modulation cycles of different lengths. He also describes subtle sixteenth-note amplitude flutter and resampling chords. Our application: a small number of dub/machinery events with distinct dry attack and darker, moving response. The response must be audible in the actual mix, and stop or change when the composition needs space. These are our choices, not his prescription for this project. [Interview, March 2024](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/designing-dub-chords-in-ableton-live-with-el-chooppizza-hotline/).

**Dennis DeSantis: change the composite, preserve the pulse.** The Ableton book illustrates an ordinary pulse combined with a five-sixteenth loop, creating a longer composite before realignment. It suggests deliberately resetting the relationship or using separate modulation lengths. Our application: keep the approved kick/bass stable while one percussion object changes its relation to them, then deliberately reconnect it at a phrase boundary. More simultaneous odd cycles would risk clutter. [Asynchronous or Polyrhythmic Loops](https://makingmusic.ableton.com/asynchronous-or-polyrhythmic-loops).

**Surgeon: coherent material, controlled performance.** He describes musically related prepared patterns, choosing among them during performance, and recording manipulated parts before arranging them. He favors strict sequencing for techno while permitting selective variations. Our application: compose deliberate variations and exposed gestures; do not treat blanket humanization or randomness as sophistication. [Direct interview, April 2023](https://www.musicradar.com/news/surgeon-interview).

**Blawan: percussion can carry the identity.** He describes combining oscillators through a low-pass gate, processing recordings heavily, and using spectral manipulation for unfamiliar percussion timbres. Our application: one original, recognizable elastic or abrasive percussion voice, varied by envelope or resampling, can supply identity without a lead melody. [Direct interview, 2018](https://www.musicradar.com/news/blawan-i-put-the-why-they-hide-their-bodies-track-out-and-was-totally-shocked-by-how-popular-it-was-i-was-kind-of-like-f-ive-made-a-big-mistake-here).

## What Undertow's evidence implies

The v003 source contains numerous changes, but its texture stem measures −43.13 integrated LUFS against −19.9 for bass. In its full-return window, texture RMS is −46.75 dBFS against −17.22 for bass. These numbers do not prove inaudibility, but they make buried detail a concrete hypothesis worth testing before adding anything. The full mix's L/R correlation is 0.9987; added width may exist mainly in very quiet material. Neither width nor a spectral measure establishes musical quality.

## Candidate review

1. **Approved foundation:** bass/drums remain the bodily foreground; changes do not erase the approved kick/bass relationship.
2. **Distinct identity:** each candidate has a recognizable non-melodic signature and a different relationship to space/density. Two mixes of one arrangement do not satisfy this.
3. **Audible intent:** identify at least three concrete moments in 30 seconds where a detail changes, replies, interrupts or is exposed. Validate model claims against source and stems.
4. **Continuity:** changes develop a shared idea rather than jump between unrelated preset sounds. Silence or a single isolated hit can carry more weight than another fill.
5. **Mix space:** inspect grouped stems and mid/high bands around chosen moments. If the signature is continually buried, fix it before evaluating composition.
6. **Closure:** the 30-second version has a deliberate ending or loop boundary; no arbitrary hard cut through a significant event.

Audio-model critique is a hypothesis. The earlier reviewer falsely described an existing two-step rhythm as four-on-the-floor, and Undertow's continuous bass as dropping out. Ask neutral, focused questions, retain receipts and verify concrete claims. Technical measurements and model approval do not replace Olof's listening verdict.
