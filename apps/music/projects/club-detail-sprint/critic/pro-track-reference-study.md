# Actual club-recording references

Two public artist-page previews were retained locally for analysis on 2026-10-10. Neither recording is used in our music or redistributed. Files, source metadata and hashes remain under ignored `.local/pro-track-reference/`.

- Blawan, “Toast,” from [Dismantled Into Juice](https://blawan.bandcamp.com/album/dismantled-into-juice): examined seconds 45–75; comparison uses 45–73.
- Surgeon, “Oak Bank,” from [Crash Recoil](https://dj-surgeon.bandcamp.com/album/crash-recoil): examined seconds 90–120. The artist/label description says the album's tracks developed through live iterations of consistent MIDI sequences before studio recording. This is a primary account supporting expressive variation around a stable foundation.

These are selected windows, not full-track musical analyses. The public previews are compressed MP3s. No direct hearing by Codex is claimed.

## Matched spectral contrast

Constant attenuation matched the excerpts close to −14.9 LUFS. Continuous fourth-order Butterworth filters measured stereo RMS; values are descriptive, not mixing targets or quality scores.

| Excerpt | 30–180 Hz | 180–1200 Hz | 1200–6000 Hz | 6000–20000 Hz |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Toast 45–73 s | −19.05 | −21.74 | −26.86 | −34.22 |
| Oak Bank 90–120 s | −16.59 | −25.92 | −25.93 | −33.70 |
| Pressure v006 0–28 s | −15.91 | −24.98 | −39.63 | −42.86 |

The professional excerpts have different low/body balances from each other, but both contain substantially more presence-band energy than Pressure. Oak Bank's low and body levels are already close to Pressure, so a blanket instruction to add low-mid body would miss the narrower difference. The new percussion can have assertive articulation without returning to the high melodic hook Olof rejected. This finding informed the composer’s ongoing same-object sound-design tests; it does not justify blindly matching an artist’s EQ curve.

The quarter-second plot also shows Toast's upper texture changing more strongly over time, while Pressure's foundation dominates except in its deliberate breaks. Differences in form, instrumentation, mastering and excerpt selection limit the comparison. Brighter, more variable or more compressed is not inherently better.

## Anonymous audio-model comparison

A randomized, matched pair placed Pressure v006 first and Toast second. Artist names and version identity were withheld. Gemini distinguished them, described Pressure as a focused rolling groove and Toast as abrasive/chaotic, and preferred Pressure's focus. The call completed with stop reason and reported $0.033862.

This is not evidence that our draft surpasses a professional record. The model may systematically reward orderly, polite arrangements, which is not the same as the user's desire for memorable underground music. The useful result is the broad character contrast, considered alongside the physical measurements; its exact descriptions and preference remain unvalidated.
