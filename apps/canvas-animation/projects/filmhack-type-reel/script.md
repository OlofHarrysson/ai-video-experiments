# Script and rhythm outline — v1

Direction A · doubt vs answer. 9:16, 140 BPM, 12 bars of 4/4 = 20.57 s. Draft for Olof's review; wording, timing and the end card are open.

**Grammar.** A doubt is always the same quiet identity: small lowercase italic in a large black field, placed low-left inside the Reel-safe band. Each answer is a different, oversized construction that physically acts on the doubt (crushes, slices, swallows, lights it). Doubts get shorter and answers bigger as the Reel escalates, until the last doubt is cut off by the drop.

| Bar | Time (s) | Doubt (quiet) | Answer (loud) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.00–1.71 | make a film in 48 hours? | **YES.** (lands on beat 3) | Groove already running from frame 1; answer stab |
| 2 | 1.71–3.43 | but I don't do AI… | **NO AI EXPERIENCE NEEDED.** | Tick under the doubt, impact on the answer |
| 3 | 3.43–5.14 | I don't have a crew | **MOST PEOPLE COME ALONE.** → **CREWS FORM FRIDAY.** | Two hits: single voice, then several |
| 4 | 5.14–6.86 | 48 hours is nothing | **CONSTRAINTS BEAT TALENT.** | Compressed, choked hit |
| 5 | 6.86–8.57 | what if it's a mess? | **ROUGH CUTS SCREEN.** | Projector-like flutter |
| 6 | 8.57–10.29 | but— / but— | **FREE.** / **SUNDAY: FULL HOUSE.** | Answers interrupt the doubts; build begins |
| 7 | 10.29–12.00 | maybe next ye— | (cut off on beat 3) → near-black | Stop-time: riser, then silence for half a bar |
| 8–9 | 12.00–15.43 | — | **A REAL CREW. A REAL DEADLINE. A REAL SCREEN.** as a chorus: each phrase in many identities at once | Drop; gang-shout accents |
| 10 | 15.43–17.14 | — | **NOV 13–15 · BABELSBERG** | Full groove |
| 11–12 | 17.14–20.57 | — | **FREE · 100 SPOTS** → **APPLY BY OCT 24** · filmhack.ai | Resolve; last beat hands back to bar 1 for the loop |

Essential message, even when transitional words are felt rather than read: no AI experience needed, crews form at the event, the film premieres, apply by Oct 24.

## Keyframes to design

Each answer gets a construction that suits its meaning; the doubt identity recurs unchanged.

| Key | Card | Construction | Motion it should support |
|---|---|---|---|
| K0 | doubt | Small lowercase italic serif, warm grey, vast black, a hairline cursor | Hesitant typing; gets crushed or sliced by the answer |
| K1 | YES. | Giant sculptural chrome or lacquer letters, cropped by the frame | Slam, then a band of light travels through the letters |
| K2 | NO AI EXPERIENCE NEEDED | Ornamental cinema marquee: bulbs, arcs, stars | The sign builds itself; bulbs chase |
| K3 | CREWS FORM FRIDAY | Woven ribbon lettering in several colours and materials | Separate strands find each other and interlace |
| K4 | CONSTRAINTS BEAT TALENT | Ultra-condensed letters jammed into a frame, contour echoes, slices | Walls squeeze; contours ripple outward |
| K5 | ROUGH CUTS SCREEN | Film-leader and projector-light lettering: sprockets, scratches, beam | Projector flicker (large-area flashes ≤3/s), countdown sweep |
| K6 | A REAL CREW (chorus) | The same phrase in several identities at once, collaged | Identities shuffle on the drum hits |
| K7 | APPLY BY OCT 24 | Bold flat end card in Filmhack red/cyan, segmented date | Segments illuminate; works from either posting account |
