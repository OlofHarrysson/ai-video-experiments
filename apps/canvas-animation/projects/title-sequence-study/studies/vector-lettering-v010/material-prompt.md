# Material direction

Use the supplied four-panel black and white lettering sheet as exact silhouette and composition reference. Make a high-end typographic art-direction board with four very different treatments, retaining the exact custom WILD HOURS lettering in each quadrant, without extra copy. All backgrounds remain pitch black. This must look like masterful bespoke title graphics for a high-energy experimental film, not filters on fonts. Top left: hot cherry-red glass enamel brush script, razor-thin ivory highlights, tightly nested burnished gold and deep oxblood contours, short exquisitely shaded extrusion; dramatic directional illumination with rich black shadows, no hazy neon. Top right: the angular mechanical lettering as stark pale silver and electric lime planar facets with directional cobalt-blue shadows and sharply registered thin orange accent rules integrated into the faces; sculptural precision, still mostly flat and very high contrast. Bottom left: the ornate Tuscan silhouette in slightly warm ivory with precise thin red and electric blue offset contours, interior engraved blue hatch shading restricted to one side, restrained gold small bevel, old theatrical showcard meets underground rave; preserve the carved counters and hairlines. Bottom right: organic swelling psychedelic silhouette with concentric sharp nested bands in vermilion, hot pink, tangerine, pale yellow, deep plum; each stroke reads as a beautifully layered ribbon flowing around the letter counters, dark purple extrusion; optical sophistication and rich thickness without fuzzy glow. No framing, no mockup, no paper, no texture outside letters, no logos. Keep the integrated lettering shapes precise and recognizable. 3:2 landscape board.
