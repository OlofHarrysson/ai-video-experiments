# Lettering board 01

Create a professional art director's typography exploration sheet: FOUR radically different, original, highly accomplished custom logotypes, arranged in a precise 2x2 grid on pure black. Each quadrant has the exact words "WILD HOURS", with no other words, numbers, captions or logos. The purpose is to choose strong letter geometry for a motion title sequence; these must be individually designed letterforms, not ordinary fonts decorated by effects.

TOP LEFT: exuberant 1970s custom brush script, stacked WILD above HOURS, broad calligraphic downstrokes, very sharp thin upstrokes, deep interlocking ligatures, long confident swashes that actually grow from the first and last letters. Compact diagonal rising silhouette, sophisticated negative spaces, an expensive vintage record sleeve identity. No unrelated stars or badges.
TOP RIGHT: radical futuristic angular mechanical lettering, two stacked rows. Very wide squared glyphs with chamfered corners, diagonal incisions, smart stencil bridges and eccentric asymmetrical counters; interlocking custom geometry with strong rhythm and sharply varying stroke widths. No generic esports logo. Letters themselves form the graphic.
BOTTOM LEFT: sumptuous ornate Tuscan / Victorian display lettering, two rows, bifurcated serifs, carved counters, astonishingly good optical spacing, modest typographic flourishes structurally integrated with letters. Old engraved sign painter mastery, forceful not quaint. No surrounding frame or illustration.
BOTTOM RIGHT: sophisticated psychedelic lettering with bold organic swollen stems, asymmetrical negative spaces and tight interlocking forms. Late-1960s phototype, curvaceous elastic geometry with a rising baseline; an unmistakable original wordmark, not a default bubble font.

CRITICAL: ALL FOUR designs are rendered only in PURE SOLID WHITE SILHOUETTES on PURE BLACK. No grey, gradients, shadows, lighting, textures, 3D, glow, or shading. White letter shapes and black counters only. Interior detail must be deliberate black cut-outs large enough to trace. Richness comes entirely from exquisite letter shapes and composition. All letters and complete swashes stay inside each quadrant with black margins. Each design should have a different silhouette and spatial rhythm. High resolution, crisp vector-like edges. A striking, ambitious typography board, not a website, not merchandise, not a photographed paper.
