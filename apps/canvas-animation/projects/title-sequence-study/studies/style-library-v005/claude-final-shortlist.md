# Claude final shortlist review

Read-only consultation, 2026-10-09. Actual model `claude-opus-5-5`, high effort. Advisory visual judgments, not human approval.

**Verdict:** the package is close to ready. Fix one surface seam in soft metal, then hand it off. The thick-and-thin version does add character, but it adds a different kind than the neon. It isn't a replacement for it.

## Shortlist (5, with an optional 6th)

1. **Script and serif** (`interlock-0`). This is the strongest title card. The neon script over the heavy italic serif gives real hierarchy, and the W-to-i join now reads cleanly. Lead with this one.
2. **Thick-and-thin script, states 0, 1 and 3** (`signature-*`). This is the closest relative of Night Fever. The script path stays fixed and only the material changes from frame to frame. The cream fill with the double border, and the diagonal candy stripe in state 3, are the best states.
3. **Electric palace** (After Hours). It has the richest ornament and the most Night Fever / All Of The Lights density.
4. **Grand dynamo** (DYNAMO marquee). It looks like a physical sign object, and the bulbs suit fixed-position material cycling.
5. **Overprint** (MORE) or **Depth engine** (ECHO). Pick one as the flat, loud counterweight to the scripts. ECHO is the most Enter the Void of the set.
6. *Optional:* **Soft metal**, but only after the seam fix below.

Leave out of the attention set: Counterform, Modular riot, Folded current and Razorwire. They read closest to plain graphic design. Orbit dial's dark purple letters nearly disappear on black.

## Thick-and-thin vs neon

- **It does add character.** Neon is a line of light; the brush version is a painted object, with weight in the downstrokes, layered borders and a cast shadow. The two are siblings, so keep both.
- **The contrast is moderate.** The upstrokes never become true hairlines, so it reads more as a sign-painter's brush pen than as Spencerian script. Night Fever still wins on richness because of its gradient highlight band inside the fill. The flat cream of state 0 is a little sticker-like by comparison.
- **The engraving state (`signature-2`) is the weakest.** Its hatching is horizontal everywhere and ignores stroke direction, so it reads as blinds or scanlines. It gets busy where the d bowl crosses the ascender, and it repeats Split frequency (SIGNAL). It's fine as one beat in the cycle, but don't make it the hero still.

## Defects

**⚠️ Fix before handoff: soft metal crossings (`softmetal-1`).** These show up in zoomed crops:
- There's a hard, straight shading seam where the d bowl meets the ascender, with a cyan triangular wedge (around x 840–870, y 330–350).
- The cyan reflection strip inside the l loop ends in a square cut partway down the stroke.
- There's a faint vertical seam at the base of the i stem.

At full frame, the d wedge is the one a viewer would notice. The "fused crossings" revision didn't fully resolve that junction.

**Watch, but don't fix now:**
- **The W reads as "lu".** In every script variant, the W's first stroke is a tall closed loop that reads as an l, so "Wild" can read as "lu-ild". The heavier brush fill makes this a bit worse. "Hours" next to it and the i dot help.
- **The sign wall still clips at the edges** (16.79 s, frame 403). The After Hours oval is cut by the left and top edges, and VELOCITY touches the bottom. It's not crowded anymore, but it looks cropped rather than placed.

The rest is clean. The frame-by-frame window at 8.6–9.1 s shows the brush path registered exactly through the cream → engraving change, with no jitter. The small RUSH at 0.75 s is the deliberately quiet "whisper" layout, not a whole-card scale; `cut.js` has no full-card camera motion. Following your instruction, I've drawn no conclusions about rhythm from the stills, and I didn't open the All Of The Lights reference images because the shortlist didn't depend on them.
