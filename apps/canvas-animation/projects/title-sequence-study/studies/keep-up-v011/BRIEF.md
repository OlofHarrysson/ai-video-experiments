# KEEP UP — AI intro development

## Intent and scope

Olof selects accelerating AI progress, investment, hype and news overload as the theme. He authorizes mapping and fixing the effective tooling constraints, prioritizing repeatability over creating a reusable font. He and the assistant are co-directors. Sound remains out of scope.

Working direction: a seductive promise becomes a personal demand. An audience need not recognize model names to recognize the pressure to keep up. The intro can suggest speculative exuberance without asserting that AI is a bubble or predicting a crash.

This checkpoint delivers reusable lettering extraction, inspected vector style frames and a proposed storyboard. Story and look remain open to Olof's direction; production animation follows storyboard approval. No new persistent goal or time budget was requested.

## Proposed treatment

Working title: **KEEP UP**. Eighteen seconds, landscape 16:9, 24 fps, silent. A visual half-second pulse with quarter-second accents provides controlled medium-high activity. Begin active; avoid a slow opening or an ever-accelerating strobe finale. Three bespoke lettering identities contrast with simpler editorial words.

| Time | Words and image | Why this cut happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | AI / EVERYTHING / JUST CHANGED | Immediate recognition; the language of a launch announcement. Original editorial rhetoric, not attributed quotations. |
| 2–5 s | BREAK / THROUGH; the full word locks together, then AGAIN appears | The announcement becomes a repeating demand for attention. Hard angular forms, acid yellow and blue. |
| 5–8 s | $40B; OPENAI / FUNDING ANNOUNCED / MAR 2025 remains readable | A dated, attributable example gives the spectacle real scale. Elaborate financial lettering, coral and ivory. |
| 8–12 s | MORE / FASTER / NEXT / BREAKTHROUGH / AGAIN | A compressed stack of announcements. Recurring shapes and held words keep an anchor; no two-frame strobe. |
| 12–14 s | AM I / ALREADY / BEHIND? | The commercial language becomes a human question. Sparse composition, reduced visual noise, short readable hold. |
| 14–18 s | KEEP UP / A FILM ABOUT ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE | An ambiguous command: invitation and pressure. Custom slanted lettering, vivid contours, sustained ending with local motion. |

The format is an editorial call-and-response montage, informed by the existing type studies rather than a literal skill-template chronology. Silence throughout; no narration or score. Shot timings are proposals, not a finished edit.

## Sources and claims

- OpenAI's announcement dated 31 March 2025 states new funding of $40 billion at a $300 billion post-money valuation: https://openai.com/index/march-funding-updates/ (checked 10 October 2026). The proposed frame says **funding announced**, not money spent or current valuation. It keeps the company and date on screen. This is a historical example, not today's news.
- The 21 January 2025 Stargate announcement describes an intention to invest $500 billion over four years: https://openai.com/index/announcing-the-stargate-project/ (checked 10 October 2026). This is not interchangeable with money raised or spent. Excluded from the first board to avoid stacking unlike figures without context.
- BREAKTHROUGH, EVERYTHING JUST CHANGED and the other slogans are original editorial language representing hype. They are not presented as verbatim headlines or factual capabilities.
- The dot-com comparison is an artistic motivation supplied by Olof, not a quantified claim that this boom is larger.

## Visual constraints

Preserve the custom-lettering quality of v010, which Olof calls much better and amazing. Use the inspected Enter the Void and All of the Lights studies for contrast, composition and rhythm, without copying their source pixels. Generate monochrome master plates individually, convert to vectors, then design colors and contours in code. Material targets are optional when a finish needs authored details; generic bevels are not a substitute.

Generate three genuinely different phrases and silhouettes: KEEP UP (fast brush-script), BREAK THROUGH (modular angular display), $40B (ornate financial showcard). Preserve all source originals and record prompts, hashes, extraction settings and manual groups. No font-family reconstruction, arbitrary text generation, new 3D engine or elaborate asset editor.
