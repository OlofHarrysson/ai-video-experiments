# Silent editorial study — v009

2026-10-10. Olof approves applying the production lessons from the two title references and explicitly excludes sound. This is a short experimental cut, not a selected Filmhack theme or full-film treatment. “MAKE IT MOVE” is working typography copy.

## Intended outcome

A 12-second, 1600×900, 24 FPS silent sequence with immediate momentum, recurring high-but-controlled activity and eight deliberately selected compositions. No slow introduction, continuous intensity ramp, random full-frame flashes or long demonstration loops.

## Process

- Reinspect the existing reference frame sheets before drawing.
- Create 20 candidate compositions from ten construction families. The count is exploratory breadth, not 20 equally good identities.
- Inspect at full size, correct material/geometry problems, and select eight complementary designs. Preserve rejected candidates.
- Use repeated phrases, unequal holds, short local changes and composition contrast to carry the silent rhythm.
- Screen the complete edit as a contact sheet, every frame around selected joins, and actual browser playback. Verify deterministic seeking, exact duration, no audio, exports and self-contained rendering.

## Scope and existing authorization

Olof approved the preceding proposal to design candidates, select the strongest and assemble a 10–12-second sequence, with the explicit correction that it must be silent. This approval supplies the experimental storyboard direction; it does not approve a final film story. The Animate workflow is applied to frame inspection and deterministic drawing. The existing project renderer remains the export/check path; native Animate sound/story-arc heuristics do not fit this silent typography study.

## Source research

- [Tom Kan interview](https://www.highsnobiety.com/p/tom-kan-interview/): Photoshop sketches, simple editing, roughly six months of work, hard cuts between designs.
- [Art of the Title](https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/enter-the-void/): designs selected and edited frame by frame, iterations around rhythm, roughly 60% of submitted designs used.
- [Hadaya Turner production account](https://hadayalive.com/all-of-the-lights/): hundreds of graphics under art direction, then animation and editing. Exact software not established.

These explain a workflow; they do not establish that more cards or longer production time alone cause better quality. Sound remains intentionally outside this test.

## Result

Completed a 12-second silent cut: eight compositions, twenty shots, 288 frames at 24 FPS and 1600×900. The exact-frame renderer has 88 unique pixel frames because several designs intentionally hold and recur. All frames contain visible artwork. Determinism, explicit loop reset, zero renderer network requests, full decode, no audio streams, browser playback to completion, preview controls, PNG export and mobile overflow pass. Twenty candidate stills, selected full-size frames, all-shot contact sheet and 27 consecutive decoded frames across three selected windows were inspected.

Claude Opus 5.5 reviewed stills/source and materially influenced copy reduction, negative space, letter-based illumination and material separation. The suggested longer timing was not adopted because it conflicted with Olof's preference for sustained medium-high activity. This is a selected assistant recommendation awaiting human taste feedback, not a claim of matching either reference.
