# Side by Side — clay dance study

Olof requests a new character-animation test, informed by Big Softie's dog stop-motion experiment, with better tooling and one or two characters dancing in different ways. On 2026-10-01 he selects two clay-like 3D characters in Blender over painted 2D.

Make a short, editable local film featuring an ochre dog and a blue rabbit. Compare a loose groove, travelling shuffle and sharply timed robot dance on the same stage. Use a 120 BPM original percussion track and a deliberate 12-pose-per-second stop-motion cadence in a 24 FPS delivery. Start with a rendered proof and inspect before extending.

The question is whether stable geometry, authored support contacts, torso counter-motion, delayed ears and distinct timing produce more readable dancing than sparse generated drawing swaps. Technical checks cannot establish artistic improvement over Big Softie; Olof judges that comparison.

Preserve all takes. No paid generation, cloud resources or publication required. Keep choreography at project level and reusable scene/posing/render mechanics at app level.
