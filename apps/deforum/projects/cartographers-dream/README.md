# The Cartographer’s Dream

A silent 75-second film: a blue ink river leads from an ivory atlas through waterfalls, amethyst caverns and enormous paper folds. The landscape curls into a world of its own, then becomes an intricate paper sculpture encircled by the river. Faster floating pans, approaches and a travelling pullback follow Olof’s selected motion direction, with a short quieter coda.

[Watch the film](http://localhost:3028/cartographers-dream) · [Video](exports/v001/p15-the-completed-orbit/through-8088/rife/preview.mp4) · [Selected cut](cuts/v002.md) · [Original sixty-second cut](cuts/v001.md).

The selected cut contains 675 paintings at 24 fps and 1536×1024, including the unchanged original 540. Its clock runs three times faster than the original Storm Engine delivery, with camera paths and acceleration shaped for each passage. Olof calls the original minute our best piece yet and strongly approves its fluid, dynamic, varied motion. The new fifteen-second ending responds to his request for more surreal imagery and a story conclusion; his playback feedback on it is pending. The original encoded film and unused ending branches remain preserved.
