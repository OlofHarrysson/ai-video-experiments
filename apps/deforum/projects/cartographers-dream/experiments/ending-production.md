## Lessons from this ending

- A camera release did not give the original film a strong enough conclusion. A visible change in the world's state provides a clearer payoff.
- Concrete material and shape descriptions worked better here than repeating broad atlas/world/planet terms, which coincided with literal Earth/map imagery. This is evidence from these branches, not a controlled model-language comparison.
- Select the earliest strong completion. Two longer tails weakened the result; branching from 7824 retained the richer relief and a clearer orbit.
- A quieter closing move can follow the approved fast travel when it gives an already completed image time to register. Olof has not yet judged that rhythm in v002.
