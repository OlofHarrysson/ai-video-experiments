# Marsh story

E01 tests whether three independently directed short shots can communicate a visual story: invitation → signal → reveal. The preferred Seedream marsh opens the sequence; two Seedream edits establish later story states. SDXL Difforum feedback animates each shot. The cut conveys the change between states; it does not establish continuously generated travel or object action.

Completed 2026-09-07: a 12-second cut with three new four-second animations. The edited keyframes establish a readable progression; the animation loses detail and becomes soft. Keep the Seedream keyframe/editing approach, reject this animation recipe as a way to preserve its look.
