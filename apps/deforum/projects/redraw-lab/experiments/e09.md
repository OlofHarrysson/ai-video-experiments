# E09 — Fixed noise for independent redraw

Chosen after E08's first 16 frames were collected and sampled: low-denoise independent redraw retains the observatory, winding boardwalk and luminous mushrooms much better than the earlier feedback experiment. Foreground gaps and subtle frame variation remain.

Question: with precisely the same guide, denoise 0.20 and CFG 5, does fixing seed 143 reduce frame-to-frame variation? Only seed policy changes relative to E08. This is independent img2img, so the poor fixed-seed result from the earlier feedback loop need not transfer.

40 frames / 5 seconds. Same SDXL model, 28 steps, DPM++ 2M/Karras. Anchor remains untouched. Inspect the same timed positions and the first repaint; compare pixel-change diagnostics as a proxy only, with no claim of full playback assessment.

## Result

Completed five eight-frame batches. [Five-second video](../exports/e09-v001/preview.mp4). [Timed/event contact sheet](../exports/e09-v001/reviews/preview/v001/contact-sheet.jpg).

Inspected decoded frames at 0, 0.125, 0.25, 0.375, 1.625, 3.25, 4.125, 4.25, 4.375 and 4.875 seconds. Composition remains recognizable; the same late lantern/reed damage persists. Fixed noise is the more promising recipe to develop from this pair, subject to user playback review.

Mean unregistered RGB absolute change excluding the first transition: **0.014424**, **53.0% lower** than E08's 0.030690 on the identical guide. This supports reduced image variation in this test, not proof of better perceived movement or a universal fixed-seed rule. The first repaint still has a larger style jump (0.028459). Independent redraw and recurrent feedback should have separate seed-policy findings.
