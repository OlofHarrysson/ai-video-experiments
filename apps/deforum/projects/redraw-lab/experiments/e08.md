# E08 — Gentle 3D guide and low-denoise independent redraw

Question: does a smaller lateral move plus 0.20 denoise preserve the Seedream composition without the strong foreground breakup seen at X=0.012?

Before rendering: 40 frames, 8 source FPS, 1280×720, X=0.002 per transition (0.078 total), near=1/far=10, FOV=45°, fixed initial Depth Anything V2 small depth. Independent redraw uses existing ImageCrop, VAEEncode, KSampler, VAEDecode and ImageBatch nodes through the shared recipe. SDXL base, DPM++ 2M/Karras, 28 steps, CFG 5, denoise 0.20, seed 143+global frame. Frame zero bypasses VAE/redraw and remains the anchor. Batches contain eight guide positions; at most two submits run concurrently.

Source: preferred Seedream marsh still, resized with Lanczos from 2560×1440 to 1280×720. Guide generated with DifforumGuideBuilder, not an evolving feedback scene. Inspect frames 0/13/26/39 and temporal differences; small pixel change alone does not establish good playback.

Success: recognizable lantern, boardwalk and observatory throughout, visible parallax, small hole burden. Risks: independent noise flicker, unfilled disocclusions, poor depth boundaries. All outputs, including the diagnostic guide, are retained.

## Result

Completed five eight-frame redraw batches plus one diagnostic guide. [Five-second video](../exports/e08-v001/preview.mp4). [Timed/event contact sheet](../exports/e08-v001/reviews/preview/v001/contact-sheet.jpg).

Inspected actual decoded video frames at 0, 0.125, 0.25, 1.5, 1.625, 1.75, 3.25, 4.125, 4.25, 4.375, 4.5 and 4.875 seconds, plus the full-resolution final source PNG. The dome, path, purple mist and mushrooms retain the original composition. The lantern becomes torn at the rim/handle and reeds develop black gaps; low denoise does not rebuild missing geometry. This preserves the preferred painted scene better than the earlier high-travel feedback result, but remains an unfinished shot.

Mean unregistered RGB absolute change between redraw frames, excluding the anchor-to-first-redraw transition: **0.030690** on normalized 0–1 pixels. This mixes movement and noise; it is not a flicker or quality score. The guide's final uncovered fraction is **11.3012%**, even at this gentler translation. Selecting a smaller move reduces but does not remove disocclusion.
