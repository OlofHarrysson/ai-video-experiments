# E10 — Repaint only the newly uncovered regions

Chosen after the E08 guide and early independent redraw frames were inspected. Even the gentle guide leaves 11.30% of its final frame uncovered according to its coverage map. Full-frame redraw changes already valid painted detail while struggling with the holes.

Question: can full-strength SDXL sampling restricted to coverage gaps fill the image while retaining original pixels elsewhere?

Workflow comparison: same 40-frame guide and anchor, SDXL base / 28 steps / CFG 5, fixed seed 143, denoise 1.0. Pack inverted coverage below the guide strip, crop it with core ImageCrop, convert to a mask with ImageToMask, expand by 8 pixels using GrowMask, attach it to the encoded latent with SetLatentNoiseMask, and composite the decoded repair onto the guide using ImageCompositeMasked with that same expanded mask. Frame zero bypasses all repainting. This changes masking, denoise and seed compared with E08; it is not a single-parameter ablation.

Risks: hallucinated reeds or lantern parts inside gaps, hard seams, unstable repairs between frames. The aim is a bounded inpainting feasibility test, not a claim that independent inpainting understands scene geometry. Existing base SDXL checkpoint, not an inpainting-specialized model.

Node input schemas verified against the worker's pinned ComfyUI version: [mask nodes](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/v0.34.0/comfy_extras/nodes_mask.py) and [SetLatentNoiseMask](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/v0.34.0/nodes.py).

## Result

Completed five eight-frame batches. [Five-second video](../exports/e10-v001/preview.mp4). [Timed/event contact sheet](../exports/e10-v001/reviews/preview/v001/contact-sheet.jpg). [All-frame mask verification](../exports/e10-v001/mask-verification.json).

Inspected the decoded start and first-repaint frames, 1.625, 3.25 and 4.875 seconds, and frames surrounding pixel-change peaks at 3.0, 3.5 and 4.25 seconds. Also inspected full-resolution generated frames 7 and 39. The lantern grows architectural details and eventually resembles a tiny domed building. Reeds and nearby boardwalk edges acquire visible seams and changing fragments. This is an interesting surreal morph, but **not successful seamless disocclusion repair**.

Verified every source RGB pixel outside the expanded mask remains byte-for-byte identical to its guide pixel for all 40 frames. The masking/compositing mechanism works; semantic reconstruction and temporal coherence do not follow from that. An 8-pixel expansion of many scattered small holes covers **20.94%** of frame 7 (raw holes 2.87%) and **38.00%** of frame 39 (raw holes 11.30%). Mask extent must be reviewed spatially before interpreting it as a small repair.

Mean unregistered frame-to-frame RGB change excluding the initial transition: **0.021851**. Its lower value than E08 does not make it a more faithful reconstruction. A future bounded test could reduce mask expansion or change the guide geometry; neither improvement is demonstrated here.
