# Brain Entity style study

Original six-second transformation inspired by the graphic qualities of [Brain Entity](../reference-studies/references/brain-entity.md): bold black ink, teal/ivory/orange forms and a mechanical opening becoming organic.

Current state: six stills and two six-second videos preserved. [Current study cut: v002](exports/v002/preview.mp4), with [v001](exports/v001/preview.mp4) retained for comparison. The palette and guided transformation work; detail still simplifies too much to match the reference. Olof says v002 is much closer to his intention, but still not great; it is the preferred basis for the next comparison.
