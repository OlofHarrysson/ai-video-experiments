# Brain Entity style study

Original six-second transformation inspired by the graphic qualities of [Brain Entity](../reference-studies/references/brain-entity.md): bold black ink, teal/ivory/orange forms and a mechanical opening becoming organic.

Current state: six stills and eight six-second videos preserved. [Current study cut: v002](exports/v002/preview.mp4), with [v001](exports/v001/preview.mp4) retained for comparison. The palette and guided transformation work; detail still simplifies too much to match the reference. Olof says v002 is much closer to his intention, but still not great; it established the baseline for the six parameter comparisons below.

The [parameter study](experiments/parameters.md) adds six controlled comparisons. Holding the opening prompt constant (P06) preserves midpoint detail, but Olof finds its motion too discontinuous. P01/P03 and likely P04 are the stronger motion references. He likes this round more than earlier studies and wants more gradual intermediate morphing. The next step follows the [architecture comparison](../../../../docs/research/deforum-architecture-comparison.md).
