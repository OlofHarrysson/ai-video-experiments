# Sunlit Sketchbook

A roughly ten-second opening-sequence homage to Shuvfx’s [Do It Big](https://shuvfx.myportfolio.com/do-it-big): a girl draws a unicorn in a sunlit room, the sketch becomes dimensional, and a cut reveals the living unicorn outdoors.

Olof approved this scope on 2026-11-10. First produce and review the still images, then animate them; music comes afterward. Use Fal through the connected plugin. Preserve the source attribution and distinguish generated homage images from original reference frames.

Current status: Olof accepted the stills as close enough and allowed the forest for fidelity to the original opening, while preferring cosmic imagery in taste. The [silent ten-second opening](exports/video-v001/sunlit-sketchbook-opening.mp4) is generated, assembled and frame-screened; playback verdict pending. Music remains later. All five stills and three source videos are preserved.
