Roads, bridges and bending buildings give the eye a route through the composition. Later samples introduce a large young figure while retaining the same palette and cosmic motifs. This coherent visual vocabulary is as relevant as the motion mechanism.

The composition evolves rather than behaving like an unchanged picture sliding uniformly across the frame. These samples cannot identify which changes come from depth, optical flow, perspective or repainting, or establish full-speed smoothness.
