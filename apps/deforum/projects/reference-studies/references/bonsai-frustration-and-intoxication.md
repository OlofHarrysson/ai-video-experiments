## What it looks like

The film has a coherent illustrated world: blue/cyan light, near-black silhouettes, orange lamps and highlights, and occasional pink accents. A dark-haired female figure links bedroom, street, club and park imagery. Figure identity is approximate: faces, proportions, age-like appearance and costumes change. The continuity comes partly from recurring hair, clothing, silhouette, palette and composition, rather than a perfectly preserved character.

Large readable shapes do much of the work. A backlit dancer stands against pale screens; crowds form a dark middle-distance band; streets, ceiling lines and trees frame a central opening. Close faces alternate with wider environments. Our interpretation is that these changes in scale and setting give the morphing a visual story: withdrawal, going out, dancing and renewed energy. This is not an artist-confirmed narrative.
