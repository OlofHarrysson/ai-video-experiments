# T7 independent review — library to paper forest

2026-09-15. **Keep T7 as a candidate for its forest reveal.** This section achieves a recognizable environment change, with an attractive final destination cue. It is less convincing as continuous physical travel: an enlarging, rolling view of the library becomes a forest threshold, but neither camera nor traveler demonstrably crosses that threshold. This bounded review does not rank the whole film against T1/T5.

**The library does become a forest**, while keeping paper scrolls, books and lit windows as material links. Branching trunks dominate by frame 592; this is more than enlarged architecture. The ending has less fine architectural modeling and more broad, graphic shading, already present in the anchors. It still retains convincing parchment curvature, dark foreground depth and warm interior glow; there is no complete shading collapse.

**Selection implication:** useful surreal payoff and a clear forest threshold, with the 584–592 cast/geometry replacement as its main weakness. Favor it for transformation and final composition; do not present it as solved gradual morphing or continuous character travel.
