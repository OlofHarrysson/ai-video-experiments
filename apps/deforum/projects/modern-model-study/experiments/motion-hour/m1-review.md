# Porcelain M1 — independent artifact review

**Verdict: useful stronger-motion candidate for playback review, especially the opening 0–5.5 seconds. The full fourteen seconds have substantial framing and interpolation costs; I would not select this as a baseline replacement from this inspection.**

Evidence: read the motion-hour brief and M1 export configuration/finish metadata; inspected all 28 raw anchors at half-second intervals, consecutive RIFE frames 24–36, 96–108, 144–156, 204–216, 216–228 and 264–276, plus native-resolution detail crops. This is static-frame and bounded-sequence inspection, not full playback. The historical `two-hour-lab-v001/r7-porcelain-travel-noise` baseline was outside the permitted source scope, so no direct visual comparison or independent parameter-equivalence check was made.

- **What reads as motion:** the opening wave visibly enlarges, pushing its crest toward the top edge while the sun and foreground marks shift right (0–2.5s). The following sweep carries the curl back left and tilts the horizon as folded terraces emerge (3–5.5s). The reverse sweep around 6–8s makes the overhang grow and descend across the view. These are substantial compositional changes. The strongest reading is camera push, lateral sweep and roll; separately controlled local deformation is less evident. RIFE 24–36 preserves recognizable foam and wave contours through the sampled push-in. Frames 264–276 retain the crane while the surrounding composition continues drifting.
- **What looks bad:** framing becomes cramped: the sun is partly clipped around 4.5–6.5s, the overhang dominates 7–9s, and a detached paper fragment remains cut off at the top late. The wave-shaped enclosure persists around the cranes, weakening the intended release into open sky. Raw frame 156 has conspicuous repeated zigzags at the left edge, consistent with reflected-border contamination; frame 192 has stretched blue foreground strokes. The late raw surface also has fine crosshatched/grainy texture. These defects already exist before interpolation. RIFE adds obvious translucent overlapping terrain/bird shapes around 207–211 and doubled wing/body edges around 219–223: the crane's arrival reads as a large redraw being blended, with no evidence here of a clean continuous unfolding.
- **Best subrange:** approximately **0–5.5s (frames 0–132)** balances legible enlargement/sweep with attractive ceramic-to-paper changes and readable composition. The inspected 4–4.5s interval softens some ledges, but remains more coherent than the crane transition. Treat this as a proposed excerpt to audition, not a playback-validated cut.
