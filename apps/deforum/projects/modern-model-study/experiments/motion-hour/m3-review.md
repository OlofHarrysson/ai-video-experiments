# M3 flowing wave — independent visual review

2026-09-14. **Worth showing as a distinct motion experiment, with 5–7s as the strongest segment.** The horizontal flex is readable, but a single travelling wavefront is less clear: the image bends and sways while the framing and subject also change. This is an assistant recommendation for review, not a human-selected winner or a baseline change.

All 28 raw paintings (0–13.5s, half-second intervals) were actually viewed on four bounded sheets. After the RIFE manifest reported `complete`, inspected 22 decoded timestamps, three every-frame windows, and native 1:1 crops from raw/RIFE PNGs. Finished source: [RIFE preview](../../exports/motion-hour-v001/m3-flowing-wave/finish/rife/preview.mp4), 336 frames at 24 fps; [manifest](../../exports/motion-hour-v001/m3-flowing-wave/finish/rife/manifest.json). This is ordered-frame inspection, not real-time playback evidence about rhythm or flicker.

| Time | Visual evidence and judgment |
| --- | --- |
| **5–7s: strongest** | The folded curl changes its lean and opening while the river and terraces sweep sideways. Individual paper layers remain legible; RIFE samples at 5.75, 6.25 and 6.75s retain comparatively clean contours. This is the clearest coherent lateral deformation. The left boundary already develops mirrored-looking folds. |
| **3.5–4.5s** | The ocean curl remains recognizable as its crest becomes paper terraces. RIFE at 3.75s softens crest details; at 4.25s, the pale ridge behind the crest has overlapping/translucent contours. The main dark curl remains readable. Every frame at 3.625–3.875s shows a localized loss of crispness, less severe than the later crane change. The patterned sky at 3.5–4s exists in the raw paintings. |
| **7.5–9.5s: major failure** | 7.75s largely preserves the curl, but 8.25s overlays incompatible folded ledges. Raw 8.5→9s abruptly replaces the ridge with a large crane-like form. RIFE 8.625–8.875s (frames 207–213) shows sustained translucent wing/neck silhouettes and smeared terrain; **8.75s/frame 210 is the worst inspected midpoint**. At 9.25s/frame 222, the wing tip and blue markings still visibly double before resolving at 9.5s. Interpolation spreads the structural replacement without making it convincing. |
| **11–12s** | RIFE is comparatively clean: native crops at 11.25/11.75s and every frame at 11.125–11.375s show no conspicuous extra wing/beak silhouette. The larger problem is already in the anchors: the elongated paper body crosses the frame, the left edge repeats into chevrons, and the sun becomes an ellipse. Deformation reads as rubbery image shear more than an independently travelling physical wave. |

The opening also tilts/stretches the sun around 2–3s, making whole-image distortion easy to notice. By 12.5–13.5s the damaged edge composition persists with little further development. Show M3 for its flexible paper/water movement, especially 5–7s; the full clip needs the above caveats and is not a clean crane-transformation showcase.
