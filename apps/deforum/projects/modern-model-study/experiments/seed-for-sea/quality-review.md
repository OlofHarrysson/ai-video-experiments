## Observations

The turquoise water, ivory forms, red vessel/petals and pale round light supply continuity. The repeated motifs make the flower-to-world change readable without requiring a caption. The approach follows the visible destination instead of applying a generic push. The later curl and pullback change the character of the movement.

The boat does not leave the original shell. The pearl relocates between flower parts, and some red petal-like objects multiply. Literal object identity and causal transport were not achieved. These are symbolic associations in a surreal sequence.

Around twelve displayed seconds, the flower separates into architectural/petal cliffs and the river widens. In the inspected dense window, the principal white curves, boat and shoreline remain legible through the in-betweens. Small foliage and shore textures still redraw; this is not evidence that flicker has been eliminated throughout the film.

The later landscape is softer than the opening, with broad pearl surfaces and some patterned cloud edges. It remains dimensional in the inspected images, but repeated repainting still changes the texture. The short cut provides a complete environmental reveal; an extended ending must add a meaningful event to justify more time.
