# Transition ramps: independent painting review

2026-09-13. Inspected all 13 paintings per branch, 0–6s at half-second spacing, in the control/lower contact sheets linked below. Times are continuation times printed on the sheets. No native enlargement was needed. This is painting-level evidence; no playback, flicker or interpolation-quality judgment. Other cases were not reviewed.

**Lower mostly delays the major conversion, while steering it toward a different, more persistent spiral architecture. It does not clearly provide a more evenly gradual scene transformation.** Control is my provisional choice for earlier, sustained environment change; lower is a meaningful alternative for preserving the original shell motif.

| Time | Control: start 0.60 | Lower: start 0.40 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0s | Same visible brass snail opening. | Same opening. |
| 0.5–1s | Buildings grow around the shell at 0.5s; architectural cavities and a city opening appear inside it at 1s. | Shell, body and desert remain strongly recognizable at both times. Surface markings change, but little scene conversion is visible. |

| 4–4.5s | The avenue and overlapping arches retain a clear route into depth; much of the right side remains canyon. | The radial city fills most of the left/center, with bridges and towers developing on the right. The environment has changed, but the shell's circular organization still governs it. |
| 5–6s | The receding arch corridor continues to elaborate, with more architecture entering the canyon wall. | Tall towers and bridges make the surroundings increasingly urban. The large spiral remains the principal form through 6s. |

Lower retains the opening's warm metallic shading through 1s, but this is mainly longer preservation of the old subject. By 2–6s both branches have smoother, more illustrative surfaces. Lower has attractive dark recesses and repeated window/balcony detail; control has broader sunlit planes and clearer depth along the avenue. Neither shows a catastrophic loss of form in these sheets, and lower does not establish better fine material retention.

Both compositions enlarge and tilt the inherited circular form, cropping its rim against the upper/left edges. Control keeps the avenue legible within the arch. Lower keeps its spiral center visible and gains background towers, but its enclosing funnel occupies more of the composition: a risk of remaining inside one dominant motif rather than opening into a new vista. The large round structure persists despite identical motion settings, indicating different generated geometry rather than evidence of a motion-setting difference.

The useful comparison for main's playback review is 0.5–2s: does lower's extra shell dwell earn the subsequent opening, or just postpone it? Its 2–6s spiral-city composition may be worth showing on artistic grounds, independently of the gradualness hypothesis. Keep control as the reference until the other ramp cases and playback are reviewed.

Limits: one scene and seed sequence, supplied matched settings, contact-sheet resolution. Lower changes the early ramp levels through the same normalized four-painting rise, with shared peak 0.70 and settling level 0.64; this does not isolate only the first repaint. Later differences carry the full recurrent history. No claim that either branch meets a general transition optimum.
