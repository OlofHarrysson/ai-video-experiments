**Method.** On a fixed 45×30 screen grid spanning the 1536×1024 image, let `F_t` denote the composed forward map. Measure `v(q,t) = [F_(t+ε)(F_t⁻¹(q)) − F_(t−ε)(F_t⁻¹(q))] / (2ε)`, with ε=0.0001s, sampled every 1/24 source second. This measures local incremental movement at current screen positions; historical accumulated coordinates do not inflate speed. Tables use spatial RMS velocity in source pixels/source second. The intended 1.5× delivery multiplies those speeds by 1.5 and divides elapsed times from second 16 by 1.5. A 90×60 grid changes the reported F1/F2 trough speeds by under 0.003px/s. Inverse-map error is below 7×10⁻¹²px; reducing ε tenfold changes the checked velocities by under 0.000001px/s.

The contraction calculation combines all overlapping scales, not merely the negative `zoom` phrase. Specifically, the area-derived linear scale is `S(t)=∏[1+zoom_i·ease_i(t)]`: radial twists preserve area, but can alter shape. F2's nominal −18% phrase produces only 3.91% net contraction because it overlaps positive zooms.
