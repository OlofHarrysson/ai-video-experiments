## Review boundaries

- Surreal meaning can remain open. Distinguish a plausible reading from an action demonstrably depicted.
- Sampling paintings can miss transient artifacts, so dense transition checks supplement the overview.
- The three subjects and motion paths differ. This session cannot establish that one storytelling method is generally better.
- More noise is not a reliable substitute for object relocation, identity control, or directing attention through framing.
