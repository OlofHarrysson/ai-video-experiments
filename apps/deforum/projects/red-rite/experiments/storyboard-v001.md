# The Red Rite: proposed one-minute progression

2026-10-18. Proposal for discussion; timing and ending are not locked. No video production is authorized by the reference-image phase alone.

## Artistic intent

An iconic satanic nightmare with a clear red-and-black identity: beautiful, ominous and surreal. Use etched obsidian, carved basalt, scarlet enamel, molten mineral and narrow firelight. Preserve small relief and readable dark architecture. Vary surface character, spatial scale and motion across the minute while maintaining the palette. Avoid fog as a transition device, gray wash, broad bloom and painterly softness.

The central idea is a place becoming a presence. Three possible recurring motifs are a horned crown, a three-pronged silhouette and a black sun. A motif changes role when it returns: a gateway becomes a crown, separate lava channels suggest a trident, and a circular void becomes an eye. These are directing ideas, not guaranteed model actions.

This is one continuous path with changing imagery. The four rows are planning intervals, not separate shots. Adjust timing, focal forms and transitions to the actual generated paintings; preserve successful prefixes and backtrack when the model closes a passage or loses the intended motif. A literal face awakening may prove less controllable than a strongly readable crown and ember; the ending must be screened before choosing it.

## Motion and image boundaries

- Keep movement varied and expressive, with one leading action or coordinated family and weaker support. A family can contain many curls; choose their placement and coverage from the artwork.
- Avoid the bend-plus-curl combination Olof disliked. Bend-plus-tilt is a candidate, not an established successful recipe.
- Judge the combined speed and acceleration through each handoff. An easing function alone does not prove a gentle onset. Avoid repeated stop/start pulses.
- Keep clear space around the leading form. Strong perspective, shear or multiple curls must not collapse its silhouette or flatten the minute detail.
- Use precise material descriptions, clear dark gaps and controlled highlights to keep the image crisp without making every surface visually identical.
- No soundtrack is planned in this alignment phase. The heavy-metal association informs the imagery; it does not choose a music genre.

## Checkpoint after alignment

Choose an opening and revise this sketch from Olof's feedback. Then plan a short, inspected opening with the existing recurrent recipe and a motion-only preview. Check the first-frame handoff, red/black detail retention, motif readability and movement onset before extending. Any change to the production generation method needs explicit alignment.
