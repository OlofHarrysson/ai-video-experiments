# Opening reference comparison

Status: two concept images generated and screened, human selection pending. No film generation.

## Question

Should the new scarlet-and-black nightmare begin with monumental infernal architecture or with a colossal demonic presence embedded in the terrain?

## Comparison

- A, Cathedral of Ember: a horned obsidian cathedral, winding causeway, visible dark gate and black sun. A spatial journey with architectural detail and room for a later demonic reveal.
- B, The Sleeping Presence: an immense horned face made from volcanic mineral, surrounding molten currents and small architectural features. Immediate iconic menace, with a possible journey inward and a transformed return.

Both target crisp, detailed surreal digital art, readable black forms, saturated reds and controlled molten highlights. The scene and composition differ intentionally; this is an artistic comparison, not a controlled model or prompt ablation.

## Method and scope

Two independent built-in `image_gen` calls, with full prompts preserved in [prompts-v001.json](../references/prompts-v001.json). These are concept references, not Krea recurrent outputs. No video sampling settings, reference adapters or finishing methods are being changed or tested. No RunPod resources are needed for this phase. Built-in generation does not return a verified itemized cost here; no cost is invented.

## Screening

Both native 1536×1024 outputs were visually inspected. A has a clear horned cathedral silhouette, nested gateways and a useful curving causeway. Fine stone and iron details are visible. Its many orange-hot lava highlights and cloud-filled upper sky make it a more familiar fiery infernal scene; the main architecture remains sharply separated.

B has the more distinctive central icon: a huge sleeping horned face with carved, finely ribbed surfaces, small crown buildings and surrounding red currents. Its palette is closer to deep scarlet and black, and the clear sky keeps the black sun readable. The head dominates the composition; an opening should travel around its temple/crown or follow a causeway rather than blindly zooming into its nose. Place small curls around the separate surrounding islands, preserving the face's readability.

The assistant recommends B for the fever-nightmare direction, with A as the stronger straightforward gate-entry alternative. This is an artistic judgment from the stills, not proof of recurrent consistency or user approval. Both images are retained unchanged; no extra variant was necessary before Olof chooses.
