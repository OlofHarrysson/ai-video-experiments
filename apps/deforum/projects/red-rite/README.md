Working title for a proposed sixty-second continuous surreal nightmare. Dominant scarlet and black, infernal architecture, lava and an iconic demonic presence. Preserve the crisp, highly detailed digital artwork and expressive, smooth movement developed in Glass Tide.

## Proposed direction

An ancient place gradually seems to wake up. A horned silhouette, a three-pronged crown and a black sun recur as architecture, mineral formations and finally a face or eye. These are assistant proposals, not approved story requirements. The film can work as a fever dream with a visual progression rather than a literal plot.

The references explore two entrances into this theme: an infernal cathedral with a navigable gate, and a colossal sleeping horned presence embedded in the terrain. Olof chooses the preferred artwork and can redirect the proposed progression.
