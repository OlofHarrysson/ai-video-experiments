# The Storm Engine

A red ribbon leads through a copper city that manufactures storms, into a cloud canyon and out above a white salt desert. A new sixty-second film with stronger sustained pans, zooms and banking, following Olof's feedback on The Night Orchard.

[Watch in the reviewer](http://localhost:3028/storm-engine) · [Video](exports/v001/p05c-the-red-kite/through-2148/rife/preview.mp4) · [Selected cut](cuts/v001.md)

Olof likes the imagery but finds the camera movement still too slow. The [timing comparison](http://localhost:3028/storm-engine-timing) plays the same journey at 2× and 4×, retaining the floating camera route and all paintings. [Timing report](experiments/timing-audition.md). A preferred speed has not been selected.

The closing pullback follows a red kite beneath immense weather. The story is carried by recurring color and shapes; silk, spars and fine details morph. All 180 selected paintings survive in the 24 fps delivery. The complete production retains 211 unique paintings, including the rejected continuations.
