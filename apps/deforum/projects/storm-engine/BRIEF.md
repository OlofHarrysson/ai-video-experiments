# The Storm Engine

A one-minute surreal film about a red ribbon escaping a city that manufactures storms. Olof directs that movement should be more assertive than The Night Orchard: larger zooms and pans, visibly greater image change, with prompts, transitions and storytelling planned together.

## Story and look

One vermilion ribbon connects a copper city, a giant turbine, a coiling storm and an open salt plain. The intended transformation is restraint → pursuit → turbulence → freedom. The ribbon may become a kite; literal physical character performance is not required. Ivory, copper, petrol blue and vermilion replace the previous film's botanical night palette. Sculptural oil painting, selective intricacy, strong foreground/distance separation. Silent visual cut, about sixty seconds.
