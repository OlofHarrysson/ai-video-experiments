# The Night Orchard

Started 2026-09-17. Olof's direction: use the accumulated lessons, create a new project, and let the assistant direct.

## The film

A neglected blue greenhouse stretches above a dark sea. Beneath its immense curved glass roof, a single amber fruit hangs from a silver branch. Its light reveals the ribs and veins around it. Following the branch into the fruit's translucent interior opens an impossible new landscape: islands of silver trees float among stars, their amber fruit illuminating the darkness. The final movement eases into a sideways drift that reveals the scale of this orchard.

The emotional passage is curiosity → intimacy → wonder. The recognizable amber fruit and branching lines connect the worlds. The destination must be stranger and more expansive than the opening, while keeping a clear focal hierarchy. This is poetic visual storytelling; it does not depend on an animal performing an exact action.

## Look and movement

Aim for about 20 seconds, with no dialogue or added audio in the first cut. Rich oil-painted shading and enamel highlights; deep indigo, silver, restrained sea green and amber. A few monumental forms and ample dark space. Avoid a wall of ornamental detail, repeated portals and a uniformly busy star field.

First move follows the actual branch with a slight bank and noticeable lateral travel. Approach the lit fruit only after its position and unobstructed route are known. Within the fruit, translucent veins become the silver branches of the new world. Let the arrival widen mainly through revealed space and sideways motion; do not append another aggressive forward push. Keep motion connected across passages.

## Production

Established Krea recurrent method: same model for opening and repaints, warped previous painting as initialization, CFG 1, three Euler intervals for partial repaints, fresh recorded seeds, Lanczos RGB warps. Source timeline 24 fps with half-second paintings; target delivery 1.5× source speed at 24 fps with RIFE outside recurrence. Use exact saved configs for each take.

Screen up to two openings. Build a short first passage before extending. Preview motion on the selected painting, inspect generated checkpoints, then change the route or branch if an obstruction, texture loss or weak composition appears. Preserve all attempts. Existing shared package owns mechanics; this project owns creative choices and explicit paths.

The amber motif persists without requiring exact object identity. The final painting is worth reaching, and the ending allows it to register.
