## Short tests

The first eight-frame test used a fixed seed at denoise 0.40. By frame 7, the detailed cathedral had deteriorated into smooth, high-contrast shapes. Raising denoise to 0.50 with the same fixed seed still produced severe artifacts.

Changing only the feedback seed mode to `increment` at denoise 0.40 retained substantially more architectural and foliage detail through frame 7. The full-length comparisons therefore share initial seed 21 and the same incrementing seed sequence. This is an observation from one scene and model, not a general guarantee about fixed seeds.

All other settings stayed constant: 1024×576, 28 steps, CFG 6.5, DPM++ 2M/Karras, 1.015 zoom and 0.4° rotation per frame, LAB color coherence 0.8, noise 0.02, sharpening 0.2, cadence 1. Prompts blend from a botanical cathedral at frame 0 toward an underwater coral cathedral at frame 39.

## Evidence boundaries

The eight-frame smoke tests cover only the beginning of that prompt transition. ComfyUI caches the shared initial image across the comparison jobs. Each full render includes the initial frame and 39 feedback iterations, yielding 40 PNGs and a five-second, 24 FPS video with repeated frames.

Visual assessment uses sampled PNG frames and contact sheets. Encoded duration, dimensions, frame count, and decoding are checked separately; that alone does not establish artistic quality or smooth playback. GPU checkpoint/resume and longer renders remain untested. `collect` reconnects to an existing ComfyUI job; it does not resume a failed worker.

Media and raw receipts are local, ignored files under `apps/deforum/outputs/`; they are not included in the public Git repository.
