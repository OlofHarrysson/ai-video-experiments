# Retrospective evidence and second opinions

Review date: 2026-09-16. Companion to [Lessons from our films and artwork](../lessons-learned.md).

The main reviewer synthesized current repository records and directly inspected selected technical comparisons, recent openings, doorway endings, recovered stills and Big Softie's revised ending. Three subagents separately reviewed early Deforum, recent surreal films, and non-Deforum artwork. This page records the scope and the useful disagreements so later readers can distinguish new critique from historical feedback.

## Evidence rules

- **Recorded human feedback:** statements attributed to Olof in the linked project reports and learning journal. We did not infer preference from delivery, silence or assistant recommendations.
- **New visual observation:** something visible in the inspected source paintings, decoded frames or contact sheets. It supports composition and sampled progression, not a complete playback verdict.
- **Assistant interpretation:** meaning, taste or recommended direction inferred from those observations. Agreement between agents is additional scrutiny, not proof or audience testing.
- **Historical technical finding:** measurements and validation read from existing reports. Those inference runs, tests and accounting records were not independently repeated in this retrospective.

No new generation, paid API call, cloud operation or preview service was needed. No full audiovisual film was newly screened at normal speed. Other creators' reference footage remains attributed to its creator and is not counted as our artwork. Work begun after this retrospective started is outside the review.

Three disagreements are worth retaining:

1. The reviewer initially valued Open Dream's returning whale world as coherence. Olof wanted discovery of a different world. Repeating a motif and repeating the whole environment serve different purposes.
2. The historical [blind review](../../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/independent-review.md) found Captive Light's liberation theme readable. Olof disliked the film. The new reviewer agrees that the theme is plausible while literal release and identity remain ambiguous. Thematic legibility is one criterion, not the verdict.
3. The new doorway is clearer and its motion explicitly approved. The reviewer finds the meadow less distinctive than the opening, with flatter shading and hanging line remnants. This is a destination critique, not a proposal to reverse the successful motion revision.

The reviewer also finds a convincing shape relationship in keyboard keys becoming feather-like structures in Open the Sky, while later cropped wings and central forms crowd the image. Local transformation success needs a separate whole-composition check.

We did not inspect every generation, repeat cloud experiments, assess complete audio tracks, or claim that present-day account state matches historical receipts.

Media links require the local archive. These ignored files are not included in a Git clone; the repository's [storage policy](../storage.md) still applies. The tracked reports and this evidence index remain readable without the media, with that limitation explicit.
