## What we are working toward

Make surreal films with rich imagery, recognizable focal forms and intentional transformations between scenes. Movement should continue while its speed and character change: pans, inward/outward zoom, rolls, regional spirals, waves and sideways sheet turns. Literal depth-based camera movement is optional. Avoid repetitive pushes, repeated pauses, crowded framing and distracting incidental redraws.

The working method keeps a plan, theme and recurring motifs while adapting the route to each actual painting. Generate a short passage, inspect it, keep what works, and handcraft the next movement from individual controls. Backtracking and branching from a preserved frame are expected. Olof supplies taste and direction; the assistant does the first review and presents a small, understandable shortlist. See the [vision](vision.md) and [review agreement](review-and-feedback.md).

## Current recurrent recipe

The opening is generated by the same model used for subsequent paintings. Each repaint starts from the warped previous painting encoded into latent space, plus noise and text conditioning. This image initialization is already part of the loop; optional reference-image conditioning is a different input.

| Part | Current practice |
| --- | --- |
| Model and sampling | Krea Turbo, CFG 1, three Euler sampling intervals for partial repainting. This is our experimental animation recipe, not a claim that the model officially recommends recurrent repainting. |
| Text and change strength | Scene-specific descriptive prompts, intermediate descriptions and explicit starting-noise schedules. Use the chosen experiment's exact config; there is no single universal noise value. |
| Randomness | Independent recorded sampling noise/seeds between paintings. Tested correlated-noise/history recipes did not improve preservation enough to adopt. |
| Spatial controls | Time-based transforms with Lanczos RGB resampling and reflected borders. Choose from the [motion palette](motion-palette.md); preview motion alone before inference. |
| Source timing | 24 fps; recent films repaint every 0.5 source seconds, or cadence 12. Other historical intervals remain valid only for their recorded experiments. |
| Recent delivery timing | Cartographer maps `round(source_frame/4.5)` at 24 fps: nine paintings per delivered second, alternating two- and three-frame intervals. Earlier Storm, Orchard and Seed deliveries used 1.5× source speed and three paintings per delivered second. Preserve each film’s recorded timing. |
| Standard interpolation | RIFE 4.25, scale 1, between paintings; interpolated frames never feed back into generation. Cartographer uses two additional final holds. Moving-tail alternatives remain project-specific and must be screened for edge artifacts. |
| Optional finishing candidate | Align the next painting to the preceding painting's spatial frame, interpolate, then apply planned motion to intermediate frames. Some turning detail improved in inspected examples; this has not replaced standard finishing. |
