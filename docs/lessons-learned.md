# Lessons from our films and artwork

Retrospective: 2026-09-16. Covers the preserved early Deforum studies, recurrent Krea experiments through the continuous doorway passage, regional composition, and the puppy films. This is a synthesis of selected evidence, not an exhaustive screening of every generation. Work started after this review began is outside its scope.

We already had substantial learning records: the [experiment notebook](../apps/deforum/projects/EXPERIMENTS.md), [Olof's learning journal](olof-learning-journal.md), individual project reviews, and [research notes](research/README.md). What was missing was a cross-project account of what our work does well, where it falls short, and how those findings should change the next film. This document fills that role. Exact recipes and chronological evidence remain with their experiments; [current state](current-state.md) owns the active workflow.

## What the body of work says

Our strongest work combines an immediately readable strange image, a small set of recurring visual motifs, and movement that takes us somewhere. We are much better at making an evocative world transform than at making a particular character perform a precise action. The most useful recent advance is the ability to inspect a passage, preserve its successful opening, and redirect what follows.

Our recurring weakness is mistaking activity for development. A picture can acquire more detail without becoming more interesting; a camera can travel farther without revealing anything; a continuity metric can improve without the film looking better. Many openings have stronger material depth and composition than their later repaints. Some endings resolve the route while losing the opening's imaginative force.

This is an assistant synthesis. Olof's recorded preferences anchor the creative direction, but do not prove which individual parameter caused an improvement. Three additional agents reviewed separate bodies of work for this retrospective. They provide additional scrutiny, not statistically independent audience research or a substitute for Olof's judgment. New visual observations come from sampled paintings, contact sheets and selected frame sequences, not a fresh complete normal-speed audiovisual screening. See the [evidence and second opinions](reviews/2026-09-16-retrospective.md).

## The lessons to carry into the next film

| Lesson | Evidence that changed our judgment | Practical consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start with a strong visual proposition. | The whale railway, lantern boat, and Brain Entity palette have a readable focal idea before movement begins. | Approve the opening's silhouette, value structure and intended route before extending it. |
| Give movement a destination and a reason. | The doorway improved when the route avoided an obstructing harp and forward movement relaxed after arrival. | Plan anticipation, passage and arrival separately; keep the motion connected. |
| Motifs support continuity more reliably than exact identities. | Train, clock, lantern and shell recur; the escaping bird duplicates and changes. | Use recurring shapes and colors for poetic continuity. Demand explicit visual proof when a plot needs one particular actor. |
| More frames do not guarantee smaller meaningful changes. | Extra shell-to-city paintings added architecture but retained a large reveal. | Judge intermediate *states*, not only repaint count or interpolation density. |
| Protect the reason we liked the opening. | Early marsh paintings and later cathedral controls lose detail, shading or structure through recurrence. | Compare opening, middle and ending for material and compositional quality. Branch or cut before the loss dominates. |
| A controlled comparison can be useful without yielding a new default. | History mixing reduced accumulated pixel drift without a convincing playback improvement. | Keep the baseline when the visible benefit is unclear. Preserve the result and stop the branch. |
| Editing is a creative control we already have. | Shorter endings and preserved-prefix revisions remove weak passages without rerendering the whole film. | Try a cut or earlier branch before another full generation. |

## 1. Artwork, composition and visual storytelling

### 1.1 A strong opening is an experiment prerequisite

The early reference work exposed a gap between technically plausible generation and the visual standard Olof wanted. Matching a teal/orange palette or a circular motif does not reproduce the reference's focal hierarchy, figure, texture or dramatic scale. An attractive source image also does not guarantee that a different model can repaint it convincingly.

The recent whale opening has a stronger proposition: an enormous pale animal carries a small warm station and red train beneath a red moon. The size contrast, restricted palette and clear silhouettes make it readable before any explanation. The lantern boat similarly establishes vulnerability and a possible refuge through the relationship between tiny boat, large wave and illuminated shell. Those are useful story ingredients already present in the image.

**Use:** judge the opening on its own, at viewing size. Identify the focal subject, secondary landmark and space available for movement. Establish that the chosen recurrent model can preserve enough of its appeal before commissioning a long continuation. A mechanism demonstration may use a diagnostic image; that does not qualify it as a film opening.

Sources: [reference studies](../apps/deforum/projects/reference-studies/README.md), [opening artwork](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/opening-art.md), [storytelling lab](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/README.md).

### 1.2 Recurring motifs are our most convincing narrative device so far

In The Open Dream, the small station clock anticipates a destination, while railway and red train connect changing worlds. In A Sky of Lanterns, warm light recurs as lantern, beacon and sky lights. These relationships give the viewer something to carry through the transformations. Olof strongly prefers those two films to Captive Light.

That preference does not demonstrate that one storytelling method beats another. The films differ in artwork, concepts, prompts, movement and revision history. What we can retain is narrower: recognizable motifs and a developed destination have worked in films Olof likes.

Exact identity remains weaker. A train reappearing beyond a doorway supports a journey reading without proving that the same physical train crossed it. Additional birds can suggest liberation while making it unclear which bird escaped. Describe these as visual or thematic continuity unless the action is actually shown.

**Use:** choose a few motifs whose silhouettes and colors can survive changes of scale and setting. When causality matters, require a visible before/action/after sequence involving the same recognizable subject. A caption should not have to supply the missing action.

Sources: [storytelling results](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/README.md), [historical independent review](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/independent-review.md), [recorded preferences](olof-learning-journal.md#surrealism-and-stronger-motion--2026-09-15).

### 1.3 A beautiful transformation is not automatically an event

Captive Light can be read as confinement giving way to growth, yet the depicted release is ambiguous. The bird's identity and location are unstable, and the foliage can become the main event. Earlier independent review found a plausible liberation theme; Olof still judged the film poor. Both records matter: a theme can be inferable while the film fails as an experience.

The puppy work supplies the same lesson through a different method. Moving from running directly into a cuddle omitted the action that would make the relationship believable. Big Softie's revision added deceleration, standing, crouching, sitting, lying down and leaning together. These are meaningful intermediate poses, not merely extra encoded frames.

**Use:** ask what happens beyond a change in appearance. If the intended answer is “the bird escapes,” “the dogs become comfortable,” or “the boat guides others,” screen the actual action before treating the surrounding atmosphere as completion.

Sources: [Captive Light decisions](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/DECISIONS.md), [Big Softie review](../apps/stop-motion/projects/big-softie/REVIEW.md).

### 1.4 Inherited shapes are both creative material and a constraint

The shell-to-city studies repeatedly keep the dominant spiral. Finer text, blended text embeddings, guidance changes and different noise schedules change its windows, ribs and surface before they replace its basic silhouette. Porcelain's wave becomes a paper ridge more readily than it becomes an entirely different airborne subject. Velvet's curtains persist even when described as sails.

This suggests a useful directing strategy for the tested recipes: work with a promising inherited shape, or move toward open space where a new form can emerge. It does not prove that any particular silhouette is impossible to replace. It also warns against overusing the same circular portal composition simply because the loop preserves it well.

**Use:** decide whether the existing large form should transform, become a frame, or leave the composition. If its persistence prevents the intended event, reframe or branch earlier. Adding the new object beside the old one is a different outcome from transforming the old object.

Sources: [prompt bridges](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/prompt-bridges.md), [smaller transformation stages](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/transition-stages/README.md), [two-hour lab](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/two-hour-lab/README.md).

### 1.5 A clearer ending can still be a less interesting picture

The continuous doorway passage establishes an open meadow and releases the push into lateral drift. Olof approves its motion. The retrospective's recent-film reviewer nevertheless finds the ordinary sunny meadow less distinctive than the whale railway opening. The main reviewer also sees flatter surfaces and repeated edge patterns in the ending comparison, alongside the successful open composition.

This is a useful disagreement about the next artistic opportunity, not grounds to undo the approved shot. The earlier destination was more obstructed; the new destination is clearer. We can aim for a future arrival that retains that clarity while offering a comparably unusual new visual idea.

**Use:** evaluate the payoff twice: does arrival read, and is the place we arrive worth the anticipation? Keep one familiar motif while giving the new world a distinct dominant form.

Sources: [continuous doorway passage](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/doorway-cli/README.md), [ending comparison](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/exports/doorway-cli-review-v001/endings.jpg).

## 2. Movement and editing

### 2.1 Continuous movement needs changing purpose

Olof's successive requests are compatible: stronger movement, fewer pauses, more varied controls, and less forward pushing after the doorway. Continuous motion does not mean constant forward velocity. Arrival can retain a gentle drift while allowing the new composition to register.

The doorway revision exposed two different failures. A geometric route could hit a foreground harp or red wall, and joins could have mismatched speeds even when individual frames looked acceptable. Rewinding before the obstruction, measuring the actual visible opening, matching incoming movement and easing the push after arrival produced the approved passage.

**Use:** plan the movement around the artwork actually present. Preview the geometry before inference, inspect a short generated checkpoint, then remeasure. A motion preview cannot predict how repainting will move or invent an obstacle.

Sources: [doorway frame review](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/doorway-revision/quality-review.md), [CLI dogfood](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/doorway-cli/README.md), [motion palette](motion-palette.md).

### 2.2 Expressive spatial change is the goal; literal 3D is one option

The early depth studies showed that more nominal camera displacement can expose foreground holes and invented geometry. Overscan helps at the outer frame but cannot supply surfaces hidden behind objects. Later regional warps and composed 2D controls produced movement Olof found more interesting without requiring a literal 3D reconstruction.

A broad spatial vocabulary is useful, but a sequence of alternating directions is still several choices to direct. Repeating the same push or left/right pattern across unrelated paintings makes the movement feel generic. The movement should reveal a feature, follow a contour, open a route or change scale deliberately.

**Use:** choose individual pans, turns, zooms and regional deformations for the next passage. Preview them on the painting and check the edges. A pullback that uncovers reflected bands can damage a shot even when the center is attractive.

Sources: [motion lab](../apps/deforum/projects/motion-lab/README.md), [overscan study](../apps/deforum/projects/lantern-marsh/experiments/overscan.md), [motion-guide study](../apps/deforum/projects/motion-guide-study/README.md), [porcelain ending revisions](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/two-hour-lab/README.md).

### 2.3 A cut or branch can outperform another repair attempt

The shorter Velvet edit retains the original's clearer sail and removes the weak late passage. The selected whale ending cuts before later anatomy becomes distracting. The doorway work preserves a successful opening and revises only the continuation. Early Marsh Story uses staged shots to make invitation, signal and reveal legible even though the individual shots degrade.

These are filmmaking successes independent of whether we solve the underlying model problem. Preservation makes this possible: a rejected full take may contain the best opening, gesture or transition.

**Use:** compare trimming, cutting and branching against regeneration. Record the chosen source ranges and preserve the complete source. Duration and number of generated frames are production quantities, not measures of accomplishment.

Sources: [two-hour lab](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/two-hour-lab/README.md), [storytelling decisions](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/storytelling-lab/DECISIONS.md), [Marsh Story](../apps/deforum/projects/marsh-story/experiments/story.md), [workflow](workflow.md).

## 3. What the technical experiments actually established

### 3.1 Repeated repainting does not reliably improve an image

The cathedral controls separated no-motion repainting, repeated VAE reconstruction, storage conversion and spatial resampling. Low-noise recurrence still lost contrast and rich shading. Stronger repainting made different, simplified architecture. Repeated VAE reconstruction introduced its own artifacts; unchanged 8-bit PNG save/load preserved pixels exactly.

Direct latent feedback removed repeated VAE encoding from the loop but did not solve the main flattening. The evidence therefore does not support blaming PNG, VAE encoding alone, or one universal “modern model” failure. The controls establish several interacting failure modes in the tested recipes.

Lanczos retained substantially more structure than repeated bilinear warps in the motion-only probe, and was adopted for later RGB warps. Its gain was less decisive once diffusion was active. A better resampler improves one stage; it does not guarantee preservation of the whole film.

**Use:** diagnose warp, reconstruction, sampling and finishing separately. Compare actual crops as well as numerical summaries. Preserve historical settings when reproducing an old control; the old denoise labels are not interchangeable with later starting-noise schedules.

Sources: [repaint diagnosis](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/repaint-diagnosis.md), [latent feedback](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/latent-feedback.md).

### 3.2 Smooth controls do not guarantee a smooth semantic transformation

Extra paintings during the shell-to-city morph produce useful architectural stages, but the final structural reveal remains large. Finer descriptions move the large change to an earlier interval rather than eliminating it. Capping the final noise retains more ribs and changes the destination. Verified text-embedding blending also leaves the main opening concentrated in a short interval.

The recurring problem is not simply insufficient output FPS. The model must depict a sequence of meaningful intermediate structures, and the current recurrent path does not consistently provide that. Additional repaints also add reconstruction and resampling cycles; they are not a pure numerical subdivision of the same transformation.

**Use:** inspect every painting around the intended change. Judge whether the destination arrives, whether the intermediate forms make sense, and where the largest semantic replacement occurs. A calmer intact shell is not success at becoming a city.

Sources: [transition frequency](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/transition-frequency/README.md), [transition stages](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/transition-stages/README.md), [conditioning comparisons](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/two-hour-lab/README.md).

### 3.3 Holding and transforming ask for different behavior

The early-settle branch preserves the desired city more closely once it appears. Its recorded redraw magnitude at three selected paintings is much lower, but Olof reasonably asks whether the ending adds enough beyond warping alone. That specific matched comparison remains open.

The doorway ending adds a second caution: lowering late noise to 0.36 made the arrival flatter than the tested steadier-strength alternative. There is no single low-noise value that means “preserve beauty.” Holding structure, retaining texture and allowing subtle life can pull in different directions.

**Use:** choose the point where the desired state actually exists, then judge what continued repainting contributes. Keep manual branching available. An automatic scene-completion detector has not been validated by these experiments.

Sources: [early settling](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/early-settle.md), [doorway ending comparison](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/doorway-cli/README.md).

### 3.4 Drift, adjacent change and artistic quality are separate questions

Recent-latent history plus fresh noise reduced final-versus-opening normalized RGB difference by about 19% in the stationary city test. Mean adjacent-painting change was essentially unchanged, and Olof saw no meaningful improvement over baseline. Earlier optimistic framing over-weighted the drift metric.

Gentle correlated noise produced strong mosaic-like colors, even with history mixing. That failed the preservation question. Olof separately liked the earlier mosaic appearance as a possible style. “Failed control” and “interesting artwork” can both be accurate when the objective is stated.

**Use:** state the visual acceptance criterion before the run. A metric can locate a difference; it cannot decide whether the difference is wanted. Retain fresh-noise baseline for the tested preservation task, while preserving the mosaic recipe as an optional aesthetic result.

Sources: [history recurrence](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/history-recurrence.md), [correlated noise](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/correlated-noise.md).

### 3.5 Interpolation helps bridge images; it cannot supply missing story actions

P3 + RIFE became an early preferred combination. Later RIFE finishes preserve all original paintings and make intermediate frames for delivery, yet large structural changes still produce doubled or translucent geometry. A transition between closed enclosure and open city can become a soft overlap without becoming a credible continuous opening.

The source timeline, painting schedule, playback multiplier and encoded FPS also differ. The latest doorway delivery is faster than source time while retaining its paintings. A sheet's source timestamp must not be presented as its playback timestamp without conversion.

**Use:** examine original paintings first, then selected in-betweens. Keep interpolation outside recurrence. Verify anchor retention and the final tail, and label source versus display time when comparing. Do not assess interpolation from anchor-only sheets.

Sources: [early RIFE results](../apps/deforum/projects/brain-entity-study/experiments/rife-results.md), [transition frequency](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/transition-frequency/README.md), [frame-review guide](video-review.md), [doorway timing](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/doorway-cli/README.md).

### 3.6 Preserve the experiment's mechanism when interpreting a request

An earlier reference-conditioning comparison mistakenly replaced recurrent image initialization with fresh-noise generation. It therefore did not test the intended addition of a reference to the established feedback loop. A later one-repaint screen kept initialization intact and separated encoder, adapter and image-reference effects; it found no clear preservation benefit worth extending in that setup.

Likewise, repeatedly probing one saved latent/noise state establishes a local response, not recurrent stability. Each output must actually become a later input to test feedback behavior. RAW-versus-Turbo used different model recipes and openings, so it cannot isolate distillation as the cause of a visual difference.

**Use:** state what supplies sampler initialization, text conditioning and optional reference conditioning. Freeze the comparison, verify the executed graph and use matched controls. Restrict conclusions to the mechanism and scene actually tested.

Sources: [misunderstanding record](review-and-feedback.md#misunderstanding-record--2026-09-08), [reference screen](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/reference-screen.md), [saved-state replay](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/state-replay.md), [RAW screen](../apps/deforum/projects/modern-model-study/experiments/two-hour-lab/README.md).

## 4. Lessons across other animation methods

### 4.1 Stable pixels and functioning rigs do not establish expressive acting

The paw-touch study retained 97.23% of pixels outside its acting regions and delivered at 60 fps. Olof still rejected it as insufficiently compelling. The new reviewer sees a gesture carried mainly by one foreleg, with little contribution from attention, body weight or reaction. That observation is a plausible artistic explanation, not a measured cause of Olof's response.

Puppy 2D supplies a more concrete failure: paw checks passed while the visible head separated from the torso. Later texture-aware checks identified the separation, and v011's inspected extreme poses retain the connection. The fix is real, but the remaining folding limbs and limited body deformation still show the cutout construction.

A Little Brave achieves stable identities, geography, controllable poses and a portable native Blender scene. The retrospective reviewer finds its greeting readable but its emotional contrast weak: the larger dog already looks friendly, and many side-on poses limit emphasis. These are fresh assistant opinions, not a newly recorded human verdict.

**Use:** after basic control works, spend the next iteration on anticipation, weight shift, gaze, reaction and pose variety. Audition a short expressive action before scaling duration or render quality. Review extreme visible poses as well as the rig's numerical constraints.

Sources: [paw-touch outcome](../apps/stop-motion/projects/big-softie/experiments/paw-touch.md), [Puppy 2D revision](../apps/blender-animation/projects/puppy-2d/README.md), [A Little Brave review](../apps/blender-animation/projects/a-little-brave/REVIEW.md).

### 4.2 Sophisticated controls earn their place by satisfying the whole brief

The regional robot benchmark improves some placement while losing requested flowers, umbrella canopies, flags and, in some images, heads. Olof chooses complete prompts. In the coral comparison, masks reduce coverage and expose more stone, but neither method draws the intended complete S-curve toward the chin. A visible mask effect is weaker evidence than reliable artistic control.

This matches the broader lesson from fixed seeds and preservation masks: a control can improve its narrow target while harming the artwork. We should not value it more because its implementation is elaborate.

**Use:** compare the entire brief—placement, anatomy, props, materials and composition—against the simplest successful baseline. Adopt a regional method when the extra capability is useful enough to justify its tradeoffs. These experiments do not reject every regional technique.

Sources: [Krea layouts](../apps/comfyui/projects/regional-composition/experiments/krea-layouts.md), [coral composition](../apps/comfyui/projects/regional-composition/experiments/krea-coral.md).

### 4.3 Our still artwork is a useful composition benchmark

Three September 9 showcase images were recovered from their original local conversation output for this review. They had no obvious indexed project home. No recorded Olof ranking was found.

The giant moth confronting a small person at a railway station has a clear relationship, dramatic scale and opposing attention. Its detailed wings serve that encounter. The red-panda restaurant inside a cassette organizes many little activities around one coherent enclosing object. The shell cathedral makes an amber spiral a destination within dark rock, reflective water and architectural ribs.

The shared lesson is specific: complexity works when one visual proposition organizes it, and quieter areas give detail somewhere to stand out. These stills offer useful standards for concept and composition. They provide no evidence that the same image can survive recurrent animation or support the intended movement.

Local originals and recovery provenance: [showcase evidence](reviews/2026-09-16-retrospective.md#recovered-still-artwork).

## 5. How we should experiment and collaborate

### 5.1 Separate delivery, observations, recommendation and acceptance

A Little Brave's portable scene, frame hashes and complete decode are meaningful delivery achievements. Big Softie v3 fixes documented issues, but its predecessor received the positive playback feedback. The paw-touch film is delivered and creatively rejected. The history experiment is reproducible and not worth adopting for its tested purpose.

Use five distinct statements when recording a result: what was delivered, what was directly observed, what the assistant recommends, what Olof actually said, and what remains unknown. This prevents a successful run from quietly becoming an accepted artistic result. Keep newer dated feedback visible without rewriting old uncertainty as if it never existed.

### 5.2 Use second opinions to expose assumptions

The retrospective reviewers independently identify strong focal ideas, declining richness through repetition, and uncertain action despite readable themes. They also disagree usefully. One initially rewards the returning whale world for coherence; Olof wants a different destination. An earlier blind reader sees a clear liberation theme in Captive Light; Olof dislikes the film. The recent reviewer approves the doorway's spatial clarity but questions its final visual originality.

These are different evaluation criteria rather than votes to average. For the next subjective review, give an agent the images without the proposed interpretation where possible, ask what they actually see happening, then compare that account with the brief. Record what they saw, what they inferred and what they did not inspect. Call a review blind only when the relevant intent and verdict were actually withheld.

### 5.3 Keep the assistant's first pass demanding and the human choice small

Inspect broad progression, then a few useful dense windows—including a successful transition, not only the worst defect. Present one recommendation and one meaningful alternative. Explain what changed and where to look. Olof should not have to distinguish six nearly identical diagnostics to keep the project moving.

Our frame-based doorway screening missed the rhythm problem that playback exposed. That limitation should remain explicit. Sparse sheets are useful for composition, dense frames for local continuity, and human playback for rhythm and emotional timing. Audio and musical fit were not independently reassessed in this retrospective.

Source: [review agreement](review-and-feedback.md).

### 5.4 Preserve evidence so rejection remains useful

The ability to branch from an earlier painting, compare an unchanged prefix, and retain alternate endings has repeatedly paid off. Keep the image, recipe, source lineage, delivery timing and review together. Media is ignored by Git; a clean clone is not a complete archive or backup.

The recovered showcase demonstrates another failure mode: preserved artwork can be practically lost if it is only discoverable in conversation history. This review saves exact copies and a provenance manifest locally. New frame extractions are also retained with source hashes and actual decoded timestamps. We did not rerun historical inference, cloud cleanup or billing checks; dated execution receipts remain historical evidence.

Sources: [storage policy](storage.md), [workflow](workflow.md), [retrospective evidence archive](reviews/2026-09-16-retrospective.md#coverage-and-preservation).

## 7. What is still unresolved, and what is worth doing next

These are recommendations from the retrospective, not new authorizations, completed experiments or changes to the production recipe.

| Priority | Question | Smallest informative next comparison | What would count as progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First creative priority | Can a new destination feel as distinctive as the opening while remaining easy to enter? | Keep the approved doorway motion as a reference; design a short new arrival with one familiar motif and one strong new world idea. | The route and payoff read without explanation, and Olof finds the destination worth the approach. |
| When a story needs it | Can we depict one clear action with a persistent actor? | A very short approach/crossing/reaction or contact beat, reviewed before expanding the surrounding film. | The actor remains identifiable and the action is visible without the title. |
| Bounded technical follow-up | Does low-noise repainting add useful life during a hold? | Same opening and motion, short warp-only versus low-noise continuation, comparable finishing. | A visible benefit worth the added drift, compute and complexity—not simply nonzero pixel change. |
| Continuing craft practice | Can the ending preserve the opening's material richness? | Compare opening/middle/ending crops and full compositions; try an earlier cut or branch before another broad recipe sweep. | A better viewed ending, with the improvement distinguished from a darker, sharper or more detailed image alone. |
| When returning to character films | Can the characters convey weight and changing attention? | One short expressive gesture with anticipation and reciprocal reaction. | A convincing performance before expensive full-quality delivery. |

For now, keep broad history/noise mixing, guidance, adapter and sampler sweeps parked unless a new concrete hypothesis addresses a visible failure. Their previous results remain useful evidence. Gradual semantic transformation, exact identity through action, durable material richness and reliable control of hidden geometry remain open problems in this body of work.

## Maintaining this document

Update this synthesis when a new result changes a lesson or supplies an important counterexample. Add the source report and keep the finding scoped to its scene, method and human feedback. Routine runs belong in the experiment notebook; operational defaults belong in current state; personal preferences belong in the learning journal. This retrospective should remain an aid to choosing and directing the next film, rather than another chronological log.
