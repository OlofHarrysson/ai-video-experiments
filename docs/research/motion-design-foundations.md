# Composing motion for a continuous surreal journey

Research and directing study, 2026-10-10. Trigger: Olof finds World Seed's revised rhythm better, but its movement still feels dominated by zooming. He suggests occasional rotation and asks for a foundation for choosing and combining motion. This complements the [control vocabulary](../motion-palette.md) and [filmmaking guide](filmmaking-for-ai-animation.md).

## What this study establishes

We have controls for several kinds of movement. The missing practice is designing how the viewer's attention, the framing and the world change together. Smoother curves alone do not supply that variety. The recommendations below are directing hypotheses for this film, not an empirically validated recipe for appeal.

Evidence: read the linked practitioner explanations and official lesson chapter list; inspected browser samples of Ordinary Folk's Minima Spiral and SOM Manifesto Tunnel loops; inspected World Seed's final delivered frame. This was not a complete audiovisual screening of all reference films. The Alan Becker transcript export was unavailable; the chapter links below are verified against the creator's description, not a claim to have studied the whole lesson. No new film was generated and no paid course was purchased.

## A small study sequence

### 1. Animation fundamentals: separate timing, path and overlap

[Alan Becker — 12 Principles of Animation, official full series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4). Start with [staging, 4:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4&t=254s), [overlap, 9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4&t=554s), [arcs, 12:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4&t=777s), and [timing, 16:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDqjIdI4bF4&t=971s). Anticipation begins at 2:07; slow in/out at 11:17.

Study question: what changes if the route stays the same but the spacing between positions changes? Then ask what happens when supporting movement begins or finishes at a different time from the main action.

Application hypothesis: maintain a travelling path while a bank develops and releases across it. Character-animation easing is not a command to brake the camera at every boundary. Arcs are an expressive option, not a requirement that all movement be curved. Our surreal world need not obey physical realism.

### 2. Direct the eye through the transformation

[School of Motion — Eye Tracing](https://schoolofmotion.com/blog/eye-tracing-animation) explains how movement, framing, contrast and color work together to lead attention. Its examples and embedded tutorial give us a concrete subject to follow rather than an abstract instruction to make things more dynamic.

Application hypothesis: choose a visible destination, keep its route legible, then let it hand attention to the next form. A flower opening could become the city's iris while staying near the same useful screen region. This can happen within continuous painting; it does not require a cut. If rotation throws the destination away while another bright object takes over accidentally, the motion has lost its purpose.

### 3. Study compound movement in studio work

[Ordinary Folk — Play](https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/play) provides short visual studies and downloadable project files. Start with **SOM Manifesto Tunnel**, **Minima Spiral**, and **Radial Delay Rig**. The first is an After Effects tunnel rig; Minima combines After Effects and Cinema 4D; the radial rig offsets properties in time by distance from a controller. Project downloads were verified as offered, not opened or executed.

Browser samples showed the tunnel's colored surfaces surrounding a central opening at different scales/orientations. Minima's curved light trails retain a central organization while their shapes change. These compositions are useful subjects for studying simultaneous rotation, scale and local motion; sampled stills cannot establish their exact timing curves.

Study question: which part supplies the continuing journey, which part supplies the accent, and what remains recognizable? Inspect the actual project curves before claiming to reproduce their choreography. Their [Manifesto workshop](https://schoolofmotion.com/courses/a-motion-design-masterpiece) is a possible later deep dive, not a prerequisite or purchase recommendation.

### 4. Find a physical or gestural reference for the motif

[Figma — Principles in Motion](https://www.figma.com/blog/principles-in-motion/) interviews three practicing motion designers. Particularly useful here: Ben Hill recommends looking at nature, film and gestural forms beyond other motion graphics; the discussion puts pacing and the larger crescendo before detailed curve work.

Our application: give each world a movement character to investigate. Jungle: winding growth and drifting foliage. City: a broad bank following a suspended road. Crystal: slower local folds against continuing travel. These are hypotheses to test, not fixed movements assigned to every jungle, city or crystal scene. Do not import the article's match-cut technique into this no-cut film, or treat its shorthand about constant speed as a universal rule.

## What would make a move work here?

The following is our synthesis for reviewing this particular film.

| Decision | Promising result | Reason to revise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | I can follow the intended opening or landmark through the move | Several equally strong changes compete; the destination disappears unintentionally |
| Path | The framing reveals a new spatial relationship | The entire picture just grows while the composition stays functionally identical |
| Overlap | Travel carries through the entrance and exit of a bank or deformation | All controls accelerate and settle together, producing another pulse |
| Contrast | A broad change of motion character distinguishes passages | Equal-duration left/right/zoom cycles become predictable |
| Transformation | Movement prepares a change and the crossing delivers it | We approach a gateway indefinitely, or redraw it before reaching it |
| Readability | A recognizable structure survives enough to make its change perceptible | Ornament churn consumes attention without contributing to the intended event |

These are questions, not a checklist to maximize in every frame. Deliberate disorientation or visual overload may be the point of a passage. Smoothness means continuity appropriate to the motif; it does not require weak motion. Variety can come from changing direction, center, local deformation, framing or relative movement without repeatedly changing the forward speed.

## Applying rotation to the actual World Seed painting

The [current ending frame](../../apps/deforum/projects/world-seed/exports/v001/p07-out-above-the-city/faster/rife-moving-tail/frames/0239.png) contains a gold road winding into cloud, a distant pointed tower left of center, and a large cropped architectural column on the right. These offer a specific next experiment:

1. Carry the existing forward movement into a broad lateral reframing along the road, using the distant tower as an initial visual anchor.
2. Let a gentle bank develop across that turn. Begin and finish the bank at different moments from the lateral motion; do not automatically reverse it at the next curve in the painted road.
3. Let the nearby architecture move toward the edge as the open cloud corridor becomes the next composition. Judge whether this reads as a reveal rather than a rotating postcard.
4. Introduce a local spiral only if the generated architecture forms a useful circular opening. Let its deformation prepare the crystalline reveal; do not twist the entire city merely because spin is available.

This is a proposed direction, not a solved path. Our flat-image transforms do not supply true depth parallax or hidden sides of buildings. Preview the actual painting with the existing tools, then inspect the recurrent result; if the gap closes or the tower migrates, choose a new route or branch before the failure. No cuts or replacement stills.

## What perception research can and cannot tell us

In a letter-search experiment, [Abrams and Christ (2003)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1111/1467-9280.01458) found an advantage for recently started motion over ongoing motion. [Franconeri and Simons (2005)](https://visualthinking.psych.northwestern.edu/publications/FranconeriSimons2005_Abrams.pdf) challenged the interpretation, including the relative timing of the events. These findings concern attentional capture in controlled tasks, not whether a film is beautiful or enjoyable.

The restrained design hypothesis is to use a selected local change to redirect attention against sustained travel. This does not support randomly restarting the whole camera, and attracting attention is not the same as sustaining interest. Olof's playback judgment remains our evidence of whether this film's movement succeeds.

## Turn research into practice

For the next short experiment, compare the preserved forward-travel baseline with **one composed bank-and-reveal passage** using the same starting painting. Sketch start, strongest turn and landing composition; preview motion alone with the existing lead-in; then repaint only the promising route. Screen the whole passage at delivery speed and inspect the turn closely. Record where attention should go and what became newly visible. Success means the move feels continuous, has a distinct character, and reveals something worth reaching. Adding a control is not itself success.
